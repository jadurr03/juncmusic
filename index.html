WELCOME TO THE OFFICIAL JUNC WEBSITE

After approximately 22 years of absolutely no demand, JUNC has returned to the World Wide Web.

This is a living archive of the band: old recordings, photos, setlists, shows, flyers, stories, and whatever else survived.

WHAT IS THIS?

JUNC was a real band. This is a real archive. The website just happens to believe it is still 2003.

Most of the historical material here is exactly that: historical. New experiments, reconstructed ideas, concept records, and future nonsense live in JUNC LABS.
